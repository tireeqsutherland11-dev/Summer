BASE — SYSTEM OPERATING ROADMAP (formerly Road)
===============================================

Purpose and scope
-----------------
Base.mq5 is a MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor used for analysis, chart drawing, and
alerts. It does not place, modify, or close orders. On each newly detected bar
of the Structure, Setup, or LTF timeframe it rebuilds its view from closed
candles, classifies market structure, draws enabled overlays, updates the
dashboard, and optionally alerts on structure events that became known on the
most recently closed bar. Both OnTick and a two-second timer check for new
bars, so a quiet market can still trigger a refresh.

Base.pine is the TradingView (Pine Script v6) indicator version. It runs the
same structure engine bar by bar and has the same inputs and dashboard; see
the README section "Base for TradingView" for how it differs.

The processing pipeline is:

  1. Resolve PERIOD_CURRENT inputs and load every required series: closed
     structure, setup, LTF and chart candles plus the enabled MA, ADX and ATR
     values and the LTF ATR. If anything is still synchronising, stop here and
     keep the existing drawing; the timer retries.
  2. Replay the structure-timeframe history (ReplayStructure): confirm and
     classify swing highs/lows as HH/LH/LL/HL and detect close-confirmed
     BOS/CHoCH events from the label of each broken swing, trailing the
     Strong/Weak High/Low. Trend, labels and signal drawings all come from
     this one replay. Replay each timeframe's Internal Structure
     (ReplayInternalStructure) for the dashboard.
  3. Replay the Setup (MTF) and LTF histories the same way for their trends.
  4. Delete Base's existing chart objects.
  5. Draw the structure: the structure replay itself on the structure chart,
     or a replay of the active chart timeframe on any other chart, with its
     Internal Structure breaks and Strong/Weak High/Low.
  6. Qualify the latest closed structure candle with the session, HTF MA, MTF
     MA, ADX and ATR filters (shown in the dashboard tooltip) and draw
     optional MA lines.
  7. Correlate the HTF, MTF and LTF trends to decide Market Tradability.
  8. Combine tradability, extension, relative volume and relative price
     momentum in the Optimal Conditions Meter.
  9. Render the dashboard, send any eligible alert, and redraw the chart.

All signals are based on closed candles (CopyRates starts at shift 1). A wick
through a level is intentionally not a break; the candle must close beyond it.


1. GENERAL / REFRESH SYSTEM
---------------------------

Structure_Timeframe (default PERIOD_H4)
  The fixed timeframe used for the HTF swings, BOS/CHoCH, HTF Market Trend,
  Market Tradability, HTF MA, ADX, ATR, dashboard close, and alerts.
  Changing the chart period does not change these results. PERIOD_CURRENT
  remains available as an explicit opt-in to chart-dependent analysis.

Setup_Entry_Timeframe (default PERIOD_H1)
  Drives the independent MTF/setup trend used in timeframe correlation, and
  the MTF MA filter.

LTF_Timeframe (default PERIOD_M15)
  Supplies the LTF trend for timeframe correlation, plus the close, ATR,
  volume and true range used by the Optimal Conditions Meter.

Trend Analysis Timeframes: Use_HTF (Use HTF, default true), Use_MTF (Use MTF,
default true), Use_LTF (Use LTF, default false)
  Independently select which timeframe trends are shown on the dashboard and
  used by Market Tradability and the correlation component of Optimal
  Conditions.
  * Enabled trends must be directional and agree.
  * When LTF is enabled, its latest break must also be a BOS.
  * At least one timeframe must be enabled; initialization rejects a
    configuration with all three disabled.
  * A timeframe's Market Trend row is omitted when it is disabled.
  * The established HTF prerequisite applies even if HTF is not selected.

Bars_To_Process (default 100; valid 100..100000)
  Number of closed Structure_Timeframe candles drawn on every rebuild. Each
  replay runs over three times as many candles (at most 100,000): the extra,
  undrawn candles let the trend, its break levels and the first HH/HL/LH/LL
  labels settle before the first drawn candle, so neither the left edge of the
  chart nor the trend starts cold. The MTF and LTF replays, and the labels on
  any other chart period, cover the same elapsed time (100 H1 candles become
  400 M15 or 1,200 M5 candles, each replayed with the same 3x warm-up). More
  bars expose more history but cost more processing and chart objects.
  Initialization rejects values below 100.

Result
  Base refreshes when the opening time of the Structure, Setup, or LTF bar 0
  changes. Monitoring every analytical timeframe also supports nonstandard
  timeframe combinations where the LTF is not the fastest source. The first
  initialization rebuild never alerts; later new-bar rebuilds may alert. Its
  object prefix contains the chart ID, isolating its objects per chart.


2. SWING-DETECTION AND STRUCTURE SYSTEM
---------------------------------------

HTF_Swing_Length, MTF_Swing_Length, LTF_Swing_Length
(default 10 each; valid 2..50)
  Swing detection of WillyAlgoTrader's Smart Money Engine (SME). Each
  timeframe's replay uses its own length (N). The chart's own labels use the
  length of the timeframe the chart is on, or the HTF length on any other
  chart period.
    * A pivot high must be at least as high as every high in the N bars
      before it and strictly higher than every high in the N bars after it;
      pivot lows mirror this. Equal highs or lows (a double top or bottom)
      form one swing owned by the latest equal candle. N future closed bars
      are required, so confirmation is delayed by N bars.
    * There is no swing size (ATR) filter: at N=10 it removed almost nothing.
  Lower values give more, smaller and faster swings; higher values fewer,
  cleaner and slower ones.
  They replace the Swing Sensitivity (0..100) of v2.37 and earlier, which
  blended a Sensitive set (N=2, 1.0 ATR size) and a Smooth set (N=4, 3.0 ATR),
  50 being N=3 with a 2.0 ATR size filter. With Base's rules unchanged, the
  two compared on real data (EURUSD H1, an index on M1 and M5, the S&P 500
  on M1, six stocks on D1; 59,827 candles) and on 8 generated markets as
  follows (follow-through: 1 ATR beyond the level before a close back
  through it; per 1,000 candles):
                         real data              generated markets
                    Sens 50   N=5   N=10     Sens 50   N=5   N=10
    breaks             36.9  35.5   19.3        31.6  32.9   16.3
    BOS follow-thru   71.3% 73.5%  77.7%       70.3% 72.6%  72.5%
    CHoCH follow-thru 70.7% 69.9%  73.3%       63.7% 63.2%  71.9%
    CHoCH -> BOS      74.6% 78.1%  72.3%       80.9% 82.9%  82.9%
    trend flips        16.6  15.9    9.1        13.2  13.9    7.6
    trend P&L (ATR)     6.0   5.3    0.8        29.2  18.3    1.7
  N=10 gives about half as many breaks, each more reliable, and half as many
  trend flips, but recognises trend changes later, so holding its trend
  earned less (trend P&L: the trend state held from close to close, net of
  0.05 ATR per change). N=5 is close to Sensitivity 50 in count and speed.
  The 14-candle ATR of each replay still sizes the EQH/EQL threshold and the
  CHoCH trap area.

Show_Swing_Points (default true)
  Shows or hides HH, LH, LL, and HL text and the dotted LAST_HIGH/LAST_LOW
  segments. It does not alter pivot detection, break detection, trend, alerts,
  or tradability.

Swing classification
  * Accepted structure legs alternate high, low, high, low. If multiple pivot
    highs are confirmed before a pivot low, they belong to one leg and only
    the highest is retained (the earlier one is superseded and not drawn);
    multiple lows before a high similarly retain only the lowest. This keeps a
    smaller bounce inside a leg from being labelled as a separate HL or LH.
  * After an intervening low leg, a high above the previous high-leg extreme
    is HH; otherwise it is LH (equality is LH). After an intervening high leg,
    a low below the previous low-leg extreme is LL; otherwise it is HL
    (equality is HL). A replacement inside the same leg is compared with the
    same previous-leg extreme.
  * The first high and first low of a replay are untyped because no prior
    same-side leg exists. The 3x warm-up puts them well before the drawn
    window, so every drawn swing carries a label.

EQH / EQL (equal highs and lows)
Equal_Highs_Lows_Threshold (default 0.1; valid 0..0.5; 0 = off)
Show_Equal_Highs_Lows (default true)
  Adapted from the EQH/EQL of LuxAlgo's Smart Money Concepts to Base's own
  swings. A swing whose price is within Equal_Highs_Lows_Threshold x the
  14-candle ATR of its swing candle of the previous-leg swing on its side
  (the swing its HH/LH or LL/HL label is judged against) is an equal high or
  low (MarkEqualSwing). The two swings are one liquidity pool:
  * The new swing keeps the role of the swing it equals (HH, LH, LL or HL)
    instead of being called higher or lower by a hair. An untyped first
    swing has no role to pass on, so nothing equals it.
  * Its break level is the pool's outer edge (the higher of two equal highs,
    the lower of two equal lows), timed at the swing that holds it, so a
    close inside the pool breaks nothing. Chains of equal swings keep the
    outermost edge.
  * It neither confirms nor cancels a CHoCH (CHoCHComplete and the
    cancellation rules skip it): it did not go beyond the swing it equals.
    A pending CHoCH waits for the next real HH (LL) or for an LL (HH) that
    cancels it.
  * Show_Equal_Highs_Lows labels it EQH/EQL and draws a dotted line from the
    swing it equals. With it off, the swing shows its role label and still
    acts as one pool; only the threshold changes the structure.
  Effect: on 40 synthetic markets x 4,500 H1 candles 470 of 14,028 swings were
  equal; on real data (EURUSD H1 2017-2018, GOOG, NVDA, ORCL, YHOO daily) 75
  of 2,148. Measured against the same engine without EQH/EQL on the real
  data: CHoCH follow-through (1 ATR beyond the level before a close back
  through it) 60.2% -> 60.7%, BOS follow-through 63.7% -> 63.5%, CHoCHs
  confirmed by a BOS 77.4% -> 77.4%, LS rate 20.1% -> 19.8%. These changes
  are within noise: EQH/EQL mainly removes hair-thin HH/LH decisions and puts
  break levels at the edge of the liquidity pool.

Break levels
  Only a candle close through a swing is a break; a wick is a liquidity sweep.
  Only an identified swing can be broken. A swing is identified once the
  opposite leg after it has begun (an LL once the next swing high is
  confirmed, an LH once the next swing low is confirmed).
  * Until then, a more extreme pivot of the same leg can still replace it and
    remove its label, so a close through it breaks nothing.
  * The replay keeps the most recent identified and unbroken HH, LH, LL and
    HL. Each level can be broken once.
  * Every BOS and CHoCH therefore starts from a swing that stays marked:
    HH for a bullish BOS, LL for a bearish BOS, LH for a bullish CHoCH and
    HL for a bearish CHoCH (or an EQH/EQL that holds that role).
  * A break is drawn only when its swing is inside the drawn window, so its
    swing label is always on the chart too.

Event rules (the label of the broken swing decides the event)
  * BOS (bullish): the most recent identified HH is broken, creating a new
    HH.
  * BOS (bearish): the most recent identified LL is broken, creating a new
    LL.
  * CHoCH (becoming bullish / Bullish Transitional): while the trend is not
    already bullish, the most recent identified LH is broken, subsequently
    forming an HH.
  * CHoCH (becoming bearish / Bearish Transitional): while the trend is not
    already bearish, the most recent identified HL is broken, subsequently
    forming an LL.
  * A broken LH while the trend is already bullish (or a broken HL while it is
    bearish) is a pullback inside the trend and prints nothing.
  * An LH broken together with an HH is treated as the CHoCH (the LH is the
    level that changes character); likewise an HL broken with an LL.

CHoCH confirmation
  * The CHoCH line ends no later than the candle that closed through the
    LH/HL (see Show_Structure_Lines). The trend and the alert change when it
    is confirmed:
      - normally when the HH (bullish) or LL (bearish) swing formed by the
        break is confirmed. An HH swing is always confirmed before any close
        above it, so the CHoCH always comes before the BOS that breaks it;
      - or by the breaking close itself, when a wick through the LH/HL had
        already made the HH/LL and no LL/HH has formed since. If that close
        also clears the HH/LL, the BOS is printed on the same candle.
  * No CHoCH in these cases (an EQH/EQL counts as none of these swings):
      - the swing made by the break is an LH (bullish) or HL (bearish);
      - an LL forms before the HH (an HH before the LL);
      - a BOS in the old direction happens before the HH/LL is confirmed.
    If the attempt fails before the HH/LL forms, the broken level stays the
    most recent LH/HL, so a later close through it is a fresh break.
  * A swing printed before the breaking close but confirmed after it (for
    example the low of a sharp V-reversal) never cancels a CHoCH.

LS (liquidity sweep): a CHoCH that failed
  A CHoCH that reversed a trend (the trend before it had a direction) is
  watched while it is the latest event. For a bullish CHoCH (bearish mirrors
  it):
  * Protected low: the swing low the breaking rally started from, the last
    low before the HH that confirmed the CHoCH.
  * Old LL: the most recent identified LL when the CHoCH is confirmed (the
    protected low itself when that low is an LL).
  * Trigger: a candle closes below the protected low.
  * Confirmation: the old trend carries on below the old LL, by a close
    (which is also a bearish BOS on that candle) or by a confirmed swing low,
    while the CHoCH is still the latest event.
  * Any other event first ends the watch and the CHoCH stands: a bullish BOS
    (the CHoCH is confirmed), or a bearish CHoCH (the low after the trigger
    held above the old LL, which is chop).
  On confirmation the CHoCH is relabelled LS and drawn in the LS colour in
  the same place, and the trend before it is restored (Bearish or Bearish
  Transition), as if the CHoCH had never printed. The LS is checked before
  the candle's other breaks, so the protected low's break is then a pullback
  inside the restored trend, and a pending bearish CHoCH attempt ends. An LS
  is not a break for Consolidation / Undefined (section 9).

Trend
  * Bullish: the latest event is a bullish BOS (breaking HH structure to
    create new HHs). Bullish Transition: the latest event is a bullish
    CHoCH, until the next bullish BOS or until the CHoCH fails as an LS
    (which restores the previous trend). Bearish and Bearish Transition
    mirror this. Consolidation /
    Undefined overrides them all when the structure is sporadic or trapped
    (section 9), and before the first event.
  * Because the label decides the event, an HH broken while the trend is
    bearish is a BOS, and the trend turns straight to Bullish. This happens
    only when no LH formed after the last event (the mirror for LLs). A
    typical case: an HL broken and an LL formed (CHoCH, Bearish Transition),
    then a close above the HH before that LL. That is "HH broken, creating a
    new HH".

  Checked on 40 synthetic markets x 4,500 H1 candles at the default
  sensitivity (6,290 events):
  - Correct level: every CHoCH breaks an identified LH/HL and every BOS an
    identified HH/LL that stays marked, and every BOS creates a new HH/LL.
  - Correct sequence and direction: every CHoCH is followed by an HH (LL),
    and no CHoCH points in the trend's existing direction.
  - Nothing missed: none of the 1,809 LH->HH / HL->LL sequences with a
    qualifying break is without its CHoCH, and no watched CHoCH that met the
    LS conditions stayed a CHoCH. 479 of 2,230 watched CHoCHs (21%) became
    LS, each following every LS rule.
  - No repainting: every event is a close beyond its level, and no drawn
    label or event changes when newer candles arrive, other than a CHoCH
    being relabelled LS when its failure is confirmed.
  - With LS, Consolidation / Undefined fell from 13.0% to 2.9% of sampled
    candles; failed CHoCHs no longer count as flips or trap CHoCHs.

Outputs
  Structure_Timeframe events set dashboard market trend, participate in
  tradability, and can produce alerts. Labels and signals are drawn from a
  replay of the active chart period, so changing chart periods reveals that
  period's more or less detailed structure, while the dashboard stays on
  Structure_Timeframe. MA/session/ADX/ATR filters never suppress BOS or CHoCH
  drawing or alerts: they describe whether a setup direction passes
  qualification, while structure events remain facts of price action.


3. BOS, CHoCH, LABEL, AND LINE DISPLAY SYSTEM
---------------------------------------------

Show_BOS_Labels / Show_CHoCH_Labels / Show_LS_Labels (defaults true)
  Control drawing of the corresponding signal. Turning one off suppresses both
  its text and its structure segment. It does not suppress event
  classification, market trend, tradability, or alerts.

Bullish_BOS_Color / Bearish_BOS_Color (default blue)
Bullish_CHoCH_Color / Bearish_CHoCH_Color (default red)
Bullish_LS_Color / Bearish_LS_Color (default deep yellow, C'229,184,0')
  Set label color for each direction/type; visual effect only. Structure line
  colors remain blue for BOS and red for CHoCH; an LS line uses the LS color.
  An LS follows the same drawing rules as a BOS or CHoCH: it replaces the
  failed CHoCH's label and line in the same place.

Label_Size (default BASE_SMALL)
  Font size enumeration: TINY=7, SMALL=9, NORMAL=11, LARGE=14. It affects
  BOS/CHoCH/LS, swing-point (including EQH/EQL) and Strong/Weak text.

Show_Structure_Lines (default true)
  Draws a horizontal segment from the broken swing to the first later candle
  whose wick or body touches the level (high at or above a level broken
  upwards, low at or below one broken downwards; FirstTouchBar), so a line
  never clips through a candle. The candle that closed through the level
  always touches it, so the line ends there at the latest; when an earlier
  wick swept the level, the line stops at that wick. A CHoCH confirmed
  several candles later is drawn the same way. It has no analytical effect
  and only applies when that event's label display is also enabled.

Line_Style (default STYLE_SOLID) / Line_Width (default 2)
  Style the BOS/CHoCH/LS segments (SME style: solid, 2 pixels, so they stand
  out from the dashed width-1 Internal Structure). Width is clamped at
  drawing time to 1..4; changing either is visual only.

Result
  Signal text is centred on its line, as in LuxAlgo's Smart Money Concepts:
  at the candle midway between the broken swing and the line's end (the first
  candle touching the level; rounded), on the broken level, above a line broken upwards and
  below one broken downwards. HL/LL/EQL labels are teal below the swing low;
  HH/LH/EQH labels are red above the swing high, and a dotted line of the
  same colour joins an EQH/EQL to the swing it equals. Object IDs include
  event type, direction, and the time of the candle that closed through the
  level to avoid duplicates within a rebuild.


4. HTF MOVING-AVERAGE QUALIFICATION (input group "MA Filter (HTF)")
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Use_HTF_MA_Filter (default true)
  Enables an MA calculated on Structure_Timeframe (the HTF) and directional
  qualification against it. Disabling it makes both long and short HTF tests
  pass and reports HTF MA off.

HTF_MA_Length (default 50; valid 1 or greater)
  Number of HTF closes used by the MA. Larger values make a smoother, slower
  filter; smaller values respond faster and change qualification more often.

HTF_MA_Type (default BASE_EMA; shown as "MA Type")
  BASE_EMA weights recent closes more heavily and reacts faster; BASE_SMA gives
  each close equal weight and is generally smoother/slower at the same length.

HTF_MA_Filter_Mode (internal, BASE_PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW)
  PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW: long passes when the latest closed HTF candle closes above
  the HTF MA; short passes when it closes below. Equality passes neither.
  FULL_BODY_CLOSE: long requires open and close above the MA plus a bullish
  candle; short requires open and close below the MA plus a bearish candle.
  This is stricter and normally produces fewer passing setups.

Show_HTF_MA_Line (default false) / HTF_MA_Color (default blue)
  When the filter is enabled, draws the HTF MA over the drawn HTF candles (at
  most 500) as solid width-2 segments. Visual only.


5. MTF MOVING-AVERAGE QUALIFICATION (input group "MA Filter (MTF)")
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Use_MTF_MA_Filter (default true)
  Enables an MA calculated on Setup_Entry_Timeframe (the MTF) and directional
  qualification against it. Disabling it makes both MTF direction tests pass
  and reports MTF MA off.

MTF_MA_Length (default 100; valid 1 or greater)
MTF_MA_Type (default BASE_EMA; shown as "MA Type")
  Control smoothing exactly like their HTF counterparts, but on MTF candles.

MTF_MA_Filter_Mode (internal, BASE_PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW)
  PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW compares the latest closed MTF candle's close with the MTF
  MA. FULL_BODY_CLOSE requires that candle's open and close on the correct side
  of the MA and its colour to match the direction.

Lookahead protection
  Both filters read only completed candles and completed MA values (shift 1).
  If MTF data cannot be obtained while the filter is enabled, the tooltip
  reports it unavailable and both MTF directions stay blocked.

Show_MTF_MA_Line (default false) / MTF_MA_Color (default orange)
  Draw the MTF MA over the drawn MTF candles (at most 500) as solid width-2
  segments. They require the filter to be enabled and affect presentation
  only.

Result
  The HTF and MTF MA sides contribute to the bull/bear BOS and CHoCH filter
  results shown in the Market Tradability tooltip. They do not change
  detection, drawing or alerts.


6. SESSION QUALIFICATION SYSTEM
-------------------------------

Use_Session_Filter (default false)
  When false all bars pass and the tooltip reports off. When true, the
  confirming candle's server timestamp is converted to the selected session's
  local clock and tested against a half-open [start, end) interval.

Session_Preset (default BASE_NEW_YORK)
  NEW_YORK: 09:30-16:00 at fixed UTC-5 (offset -300 minutes).
  LONDON:    08:00-17:00 at UTC+0.
  TOKYO:     09:00-15:00 at UTC+9 (+540).
  SYDNEY:    08:00-17:00 at UTC+10 (+600).
  CUSTOM:    uses Custom_Session and Custom_UTC_Offset_Minutes.
  24X7:      every timestamp passes.

  Preset offsets are fixed: daylight-saving time is not automatically applied.
  Users must adjust relevant offsets/configuration when seasonal time changes
  matter.

Custom_Session (default "0930-1600")
  CUSTOM-only HHMM-HHMM interval. Invalid shape or invalid hours/minutes makes
  every checked bar fail. Overnight ranges are supported (for example,
  2200-0600). Equal start/end means all day.

Custom_UTC_Offset_Minutes (default 0)
  UTC offset of the custom session clock. Positive values are east of UTC.

Server_UTC_Offset_Minutes (default 0)
  UTC offset of broker server timestamps. Conversion is:
    local time = server time + (session offset - server offset).
  A wrong value shifts the tested window and can incorrectly pass/block bars.

Result
  Session pass is combined with the HTF and MTF MA directions. It affects the tooltip's
  filter qualification only, not structure drawing or alerts.


7. ADX STRENGTH QUALIFICATION SYSTEM
------------------------------------

Use_ADX_Filter (default true)
  Enables the main ADX buffer on Structure_Timeframe. Disabling it passes all
  ADX tests and reports OFF.

ADX_Length (default 14; valid 1 or greater)
  ADX lookback. A shorter length reacts more quickly and erratically; a longer
  length is smoother and slower. ADX measures strength, not bull/bear direction.

ADX_Minimum (internal, 25.0)
  A bar passes when ADX is valid and >= this value. Raising it requires stronger
  trends and blocks more setups; lowering it admits weaker/ranging conditions.
  
Apply_ADX_Filter_To (internal, BASE_BOS_ONLY)
  BOS_ONLY: ADX applies to BOS qualification; CHoCH ignores ADX.
  BOS_AND_CHOCH: both event types require the ADX pass.
  ATR, MA, and session requirements remain independent.

Result
  The ADX reading appears in the Market Tradability tooltip and feeds its
  BOS/CHoCH filter results. It does not suppress confirmed event drawings or
  alerts.


8. ATR VOLATILITY QUALIFICATION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------

Use_ATR_Filter (default true)
  Enables ATR on Structure_Timeframe. Disabling it passes volatility checks and
  reports OFF.

ATR_Length (default 14; valid 1 or greater)
  ATR lookback. Shorter reacts faster to volatility changes; longer is smoother.
  The same length is used for the LTF ATR of the Optimal Conditions Meter.

ATR_Filter_Mode (internal, BASE_ATR_MINIMUM)
  MINIMUM: passes ATR >= ATR_Minimum.
  MAXIMUM: passes ATR <= ATR_Maximum.
  RANGE:   passes ATR_Minimum <= ATR <= ATR_Maximum.

ATR_Minimum (internal, 1.0) / ATR_Maximum (internal, 10.0)
  Raw symbol-price values, not points, pips, or percentages.

Result
  ATR qualification applies to both BOS and CHoCH tooltip filter results,
  regardless of ADX scope. It does not suppress event drawing or alerts.


9. MARKET TREND AND TRADABILITY SYSTEM
--------------------------------------

Base independently replays confirmed structure on Structure_Timeframe (HTF),
Setup_Entry_Timeframe (MTF), and LTF_Timeframe, each over the same elapsed
time plus the 3x warm-up. Every trend follows section 2:

  Bullish:                   the latest event is a bullish BOS.
  Bullish Transition:        the latest event is a bullish CHoCH; the next
                             bullish BOS makes it Bullish.
  Bearish:                   the latest event is a bearish BOS.
  Bearish Transition:        the latest event is a bearish CHoCH.
  Consolidation / Undefined: either condition below holds, or there is no
                             event in the replay yet. It has no direction.

Consolidation / Undefined conditions (ClassifyConsolidation, evaluated on the
replay's current state):
  1. Sporadic 5-label sequence: the BOS/CHoCH breaks since the oldest of the
     last SPORADIC_LABELS (5) HH/HL/LH/LL labels change direction at least
     SPORADIC_FLIPS (2) times, e.g. bullish, bearish, bullish. A single flip
     is an ordinary change of character and stays a Transition; counting it
     would hide almost every Transition behind the previous trend's breaks.
  2. Multiple CHoCHs without BoS (the CHoCH trap): at least two CHoCHs since
     the last BOS that confirmed its direction (a BOS following an event in
     the same direction), the latest within CHOCH_TRAP_AREA_ATR (4.0) ATR of
     an earlier one, i.e. in the same horizontal price area. A BOS that
     confirms the latest CHoCH ends the trap. A BOS against the latest CHoCH
     does not end it, and a later CHoCH in the same area keeps it.

A broken LH/HL changes nothing until its CHoCH is confirmed. New swings that
break nothing never change a trend, so an ordinary pullback cannot turn an
established trend transitional.

Strong/Weak High/Low
Show_Strong_Weak_High_Low (default true, input group "Swing Detection")
  From SME, trailed inside ReplayStructure (TrailExtremes) after each candle:
  * A break on the candle (a new BOS or confirmed CHoCH, or an LS confirmed
    on it) protects the latest swing on the other side of the resulting
    trend: in a bullish trend the latest swing low, in a bearish trend the
    latest swing high.
  * While the trend is bullish the trailing high follows every higher candle
    high; while it is bearish the trailing low follows every lower low.
  * Bullish trend: Strong Low (the protected swing, which holds the trend) /
    Weak High (the trailing extreme, the next target). Bearish trend: Strong
    High / Weak Low. Before the first break both are Weak and unset.
  * Each Market Trend's breakdown lists them ("Strong/Weak:"). On the chart
    they are dashed width-1 lines (IndianRed high, Teal low) from the
    extreme's candle to 20 candles right of the latest closed candle, with
    the name just right of the line end.

Internal Structure (input group "Internal Structure")
Show_Internal_Structure (default true)
Show_Internal_On_Dashboard (default true)
HTF_Internal_Length, MTF_Internal_Length, LTF_Internal_Length (default 5 each;
valid 2..50)
Internal_Bullish_Color (default C'8,153,129') / Internal_Bearish_Color
(default C'242,54,69')
  SME's internal structure (ReplayInternalStructure): minor structure inside
  the swings from pivots of the Internal Structure Length (same pivot rule
  as the swings). It never changes the Market Trend, Tradability, Optimal
  Conditions or alerts.
  * Each internal pivot high (low) becomes the internal high (low) level.
  * The first close above the internal high is an internal bullish BOS, or an
    internal bullish CHoCH when the internal trend was bearish; bearish
    mirrors this. Each level is broken once, and the break sets the internal
    trend.
  * On the chart (DrawInternalBreaks): a dashed width-1 line from the
    internal pivot to the first candle that touches its level (the candle
    that closed through it at the latest, as for Show_Structure_Lines), and
    a caption in
    the same colour faded 30% towards the chart background, centred on the
    line like the main captions. A break is drawn when its closing candle is
    inside the drawn window. An internal pivot that is also a swing of the
    main structure is skipped, because the main structure draws that level.
    Keep the length below the Swing Detection Length: at or above it almost
    every internal pivot is a main swing and almost nothing is drawn.
  * Show_Internal_On_Dashboard adds an Internal Structure row under each
    Market Trend (section 10).

Market Tradability prints Tradable when:
  * the HTF trend is established (Bullish or Bearish, latest break a BOS, not
    Consolidation / Undefined). This applies even when HTF is not selected for
    correlation;
  * every selected trend timeframe has a direction and they all agree;
  * a selected LTF is itself established. A selected MTF may be transitional.
Otherwise it prints Not Tradable. The Tradability Reason row is one sentence
that names the timeframes (H4, H1, M15 for the defaults):
  Tradable          "H4 and H1 are both bullish, and the H4 trend is
                    confirmed by a BOS." (with LTF selected: "..., as is the
                    M15 trend.")
  No break yet      "H4 has no structure break yet."
  Consolidation     "H4 is ranging (breaks in both directions over its last 5
                    swings)." or "... (repeated CHoCHs in one area with no
                    BOS)." It checks the HTF, then the MTF, then the LTF.
  HTF transitional  "H4 is only in a bullish transition (a CHoCH not yet
                    confirmed by a BOS)."
  Conflict          "H4 is bullish but H1 is bearish."
  LTF transitional  "M15 is only in a bearish transition (...)."
Healthy Extension also needs an HTF direction. Optimal Conditions remains a
separate quality meter and does not override tradability.

--------------------

The dashboard has no background and no border. Every component name is
black and bold (for example "Market Trend (H4):"); only the outputs are
coloured. Rows, top to bottom:
  HTF / MTF / LTF Market Trend  One row per timeframe selected in Trend
                                Analysis Timeframes: Bullish / Bullish
                                Transition (green), Bearish / Bearish
                                Transition (red), or Consolidation / Undefined
                                (grey). The row's tooltip is its breakdown
                                (below).
  Internal Structure            indented under each Market Trend when
                                Show_Internal_On_Dashboard is on: Bullish
                                (green), Bearish (red) or Undefined (grey),

                                "Bullish (CHoCH)". The tooltip gives the break
                                that set the internal trend and whether it
                                agrees with the Market Trend (section 9).
  Market Tradability            Tradable (green) or Not Tradable (red). The
                                tooltip holds the entry-filter diagnostics.
  Tradability Reason            one sentence: why the selected timeframes

                                (section 9).
  Trade Recommendations         one action for the HTF trend and the price
                                that decides it (below). When the HTF trend is
                                established but a selected lower timeframe
                                holds Tradable back, it names that timeframe

                                M15 to confirm with a bullish BOS before
                                buying."
  Optimal Conditions            OPTIMAL (green) or NOT OPTIMAL (red), then one
                                row per selected requirement with PASS (green)
                                or BLOCKED (red). The tooltip is the reason.
Disabled timeframes and optimal-condition requirements are omitted rather
than displayed as inactive rows.

Each Market Trend tooltip is that timeframe's breakdown:
  Current Trend Classification Bullish / Bearish / Bullish Transition /

  Trigger Condition Met        Sporadic 5-label sequence, Multiple CHoCHs
                               without BoS, clear trending structure, or clear

                               oldest of them (sporadic), the trapped CHoCHs

available on hover without cluttering the chart. Only the latest candle is
qualified; the filters never change structure, trend, tradability or alerts.


11. OPTIMAL CONDITIONS METER
----------------------------

The **Optimal Conditions** input group exposes one Use_*_For_Optimal switch
for each of the four requirements below. OPTIMAL is printed only when every
selected requirement passes, and at least one switch must remain enabled.
Unselected requirements neither affect the result nor appear on the
dashboard. Tuning values are internal and hidden from the Inputs dialog.

Timeframe Correlation (Use_Timeframe_Correlation_For_Optimal, default true)
  Passes exactly when Market Tradability is Tradable (section 9).

Healthy Extension (Use_Healthy_Extension_For_Optimal, default false)
  Measured in the HTF trend direction, which is the trading direction. With a
  bullish HTF trend the anchor is the latest LTF swing low and it must be an
  HL; the extension is (LTF close - HL) / LTF ATR. With a bearish HTF trend the
  anchor is the latest LTF swing high, which must be an LH, and the extension
  is (LH - LTF close) / LTF ATR. It passes from 0 through
  Maximum_Extension_ATR (3.0). No HTF direction, an LL/HH anchor, price beyond
  the anchor, or a missing ATR fails it.

Good Market Volume (Use_Market_Volume_For_Optimal, default true)
  The newest closed LTF candle's tick volume is divided by the average of the
  preceding Volume_Average_Length candles (20). It passes from
  Volume_Minimum_Ratio (0.50) through Volume_Maximum_Ratio (2.00), inclusive.
  This rejects both inactive conditions and volume spikes. MT5 tick volume is
  used because centralized real volume is not consistently available.

Good Price Momentum (Use_Price_Momentum_For_Optimal, default false)
  The newest closed LTF candle's true range divided by the average true range
  of the preceding Momentum_Average_Length candles (20). True range includes
  gaps from the preceding close. It passes from Momentum_Minimum_Ratio (0.50)
  through Momentum_Maximum_Ratio (2.00), inclusive. Below the band price is
  sluggish; above it price would have to be chased.

Failure reasons
  NOT OPTIMAL is accompanied by every failed selected requirement: trends
  that do not correlate, overextension or a missing corrective anchor, volume
  that is too low or too high, and momentum that is too low or too high.


12. ALERT SYSTEM
----------------

Enable_Popup_Alerts (default false)
  Calls MT5 Alert with "<symbol> <structure timeframe> <signal>", for example
  "EURUSD H1 BOS bullish".

Enable_Push_Notifications (default false)
  Sends the same text through SendNotification; terminal notification settings
  must also be configured for delivery.

Eligibility and deduplication
  Alerts occur only after initialization, only during a new-bar rebuild, and
  only for events that became known on the most recently closed structure
  candle. A CHoCH is alerted when it is confirmed. When a second break
  confirms a CHoCH, both events arrive on the same candle and are sent as one
  message ("CHoCH bullish + BOS bullish"). An LS alerts when it is confirmed,
  before the old trend's BOS on the same candle ("LS (bullish CHoCH failed) +
  BOS bearish"). A static last-alert time prevents a
  second alert for the same or an older candle. Display toggles and the
  qualification filters do not gate alerts.


13. INTERNAL VARIABLES AND RESOURCE LIFECYCLE
---------------------------------------------

Persistent globals
  g_prefix             Unique "BASE_<chart ID>_" object-name prefix.
  g_last_ltf_bar       Current open LTF bar time used for refresh gating.
  g_last_structure_bar Current open structure bar time used for refresh gating.
  g_last_setup_bar     Current open setup bar time used for refresh gating.
  g_htf_ma_handle      HTF (structure-timeframe) MA handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_mtf_ma_handle      MTF (setup-timeframe) MA handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_adx_handle         Structure-timeframe ADX handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_atr_handle         Structure-timeframe ATR handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_ltf_atr_handle     LTF ATR handle used by the conditions meter.

Structure state (BASE_STRUCTURE_STATE, rebuilt by every replay)
  direction            +1 bullish, -1 bearish, 0 not yet established.
  last_break_was_bos   False after a CHoCH until the next BOS.
  last_high/last_low   Newest accepted swing levels and times.
  last_high_kind       +1 HH, -1 LH, 0 untyped.
  last_low_kind        +1 LL, -1 HL, 0 untyped.
  trail_high/trail_low Strong/Weak High/Low (TrailExtremes) and their times.

Internal structure (BASE_INTERNAL_STRUCTURE, rebuilt by ReplayInternalStructure)
  trend                +1 bullish, -1 bearish, 0 before the first internal
                       break.
  last_choch           The latest internal break was a CHoCH.
  high/low             The latest internal pivot high and low, each broken
                       once.

Replay-local levels (ReplayStructure)
  hh / ll              Most recent unbroken HH / LL: a close through it is a
                       BOS. For an EQH/EQL the level is the pool's outer
                       edge.
  lh / hl              Most recent unbroken LH / HL: a close through it starts
                       a CHoCH unless the trend already points that way.
  choch                The CHoCH waiting for its HH->HL (LL->LH) swings.

Changing globals directly is not a supported user control; they are derived
state. On initialization, Base validates bounded inputs (printing the invalid
input and its valid range to the Experts tab), forgets the bar times of any
previous chart (MT5 keeps EA globals across a timeframe change), creates only
required indicator handles, starts a two-second timer, and attempts a
non-alerting build. If history or indicator data are still synchronizing (as
commonly happens just after a chart timeframe change), the incomplete build
retains the prior display and does not consume the current bar; the timer
retries it every two seconds until all required data are ready. On
deinitialization Base kills the timer, releases every created handle, deletes
only prefixed objects, and clears the chart comment.


14. PRACTICAL INTERACTION MAP
-----------------------------

  Change structure frequency:
    Structure_Timeframe, the three Swing Detection Length inputs,
    Bars_To_Process.

  Change which swings are equal (EQH/EQL) and therefore some break levels:
    Equal_Highs_Lows_Threshold.

  Change the Internal Structure (its dashboard rows and dashed breaks only):
    the three Internal Structure Length inputs.

  Change whether the latest directional setup qualifies:
    HTF MA and MTF MA settings, session settings, ADX settings, ATR settings.

  Change the structural Tradable result:
    Only the confirmed swing/break sequence (indirectly affected by the three
    timeframes, the Swing Detection Length inputs, available history) and the three
    Trend Analysis Timeframes switches (Use_HTF, Use_MTF, Use_LTF).

  Change the Optimal Conditions result:
    The Tradable result and the four Use_*_For_Optimal switches.

  Change visuals only:
    Show toggles (including Show_Equal_Highs_Lows, Show_Internal_Structure,
    Show_Internal_On_Dashboard and Show_Strong_Weak_High_Low), colors,
    Label_Size, line styles/widths.

  Change notifications only:
    Enable_Popup_Alerts and Enable_Push_Notifications.

No parameter turns Base into an automated trading system. Its results are chart
objects, dashboard text, and optional notifications; execution decisions and
risk management remain outside this EA.
