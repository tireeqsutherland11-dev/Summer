ROAD — SYSTEM OPERATING ROADMAP
================================

Purpose and scope
-----------------
Road.mq5 is a MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor used for analysis, chart drawing, and
alerts. It does not place, modify, or close orders. On each newly detected bar
of the Structure, Setup, or LTF timeframe it rebuilds its view from closed
candles, classifies market structure, draws enabled overlays, updates the
dashboard, and optionally alerts on a structure event confirmed by the most
recently closed bar. Both OnTick and a two-second timer check for new bars, so a
quiet market can still trigger a refresh.

The processing pipeline is:

  1. Resolve PERIOD_CURRENT inputs and load closed structure candles.
  2. Load any enabled MA, ADX, and ATR indicator buffers.
  3. Delete Road's existing chart objects and replay the selected history.
  4. Confirm and classify structure-timeframe swing highs/lows for analytics,
     then independently draw structure from the active chart timeframe.
  5. Detect close-confirmed BOS/CHoCH events and reconstruct current bias.
  6. Calculate session, MA, ADX, and ATR qualification states.
  7. Draw optional MA lines, current swing levels, higher-timeframe market
     boundaries, and trendline zones.
  8. Correlate HTF, MTF, and LTF direction and require an LTF continuation BOS
     to decide whether the market is tradeable.
  9. Combine bias, boundary clearance, extension, relative volume, and relative
     price momentum in the Optimal Conditions Meter.
 10. Render the dashboard, send any eligible alert, and redraw the chart.

All signals are based on closed candles (CopyRates starts at shift 1). A wick
through a level is intentionally not a break; the candle must close beyond it.


1. GENERAL / REFRESH SYSTEM
---------------------------

Boundary_Timeframe (default PERIOD_H4)
  The common higher timeframe for Market High/Low and trendline zones.
  Boundary_Lookback_Bars and Trendline_Bars_To_Apply therefore always count H4
  bars by default, regardless of the active chart timeframe.

Structure_Timeframe (default PERIOD_H1)
  The fixed timeframe used for analytical swings, BOS/CHoCH, Market Bias,
  Market Tradeability, the Optimal Conditions Meter, structure MA, ADX, ATR,
  dashboard close, and alerts. Changing the chart period
  does not change these results. PERIOD_CURRENT remains available as an
  explicit opt-in to chart-dependent analysis.

Setup_Entry_Timeframe (default PERIOD_M15)
  Drives the independent MTF/setup bias used in timeframe correlation.

LTF_Timeframe (default PERIOD_M5)
  Supplies the final confirmation bias for timeframe correlation. A definite
  directional LTF BOS is required when Use_LTF_For_Tradeability is enabled,
  while matching HTF and MTF biases may be transitional.

Use_HTF_For_Tradeability / Use_MTF_For_Tradeability /
Use_LTF_For_Tradeability (all default true)
  Independently select the timeframe biases used by Market Tradeability and
  the correlation component of Optimal Conditions. Enabled biases must be
  directional and agree. When LTF is enabled, its latest break must also be a
  BOS. At least one tradeability timeframe must be enabled; initialization
  rejects a configuration with all three disabled. Biases remain visible on
  the dashboard even when excluded from tradeability.

Bars_To_Process (default 100; valid 100 or greater)
  Requested count of closed Structure_Timeframe candles replayed on every
  rebuild. The runtime caps it at 100,000. More bars expose more historical pivots/signals
  and give the state machine more context, but cost more processing and chart
  objects. Fewer bars are faster but may change the first known pivots and thus
  the reconstructed structure. Initialization rejects values below 100.

Result
  Road refreshes when the opening time of the Structure, Setup, or LTF bar 0
  changes. Monitoring every analytical timeframe also supports nonstandard
  timeframe combinations where the LTF is not the fastest source. The first
  initialization rebuild never alerts; later new-bar rebuilds may alert. Its
  object prefix contains the chart ID, isolating its objects per chart.


2. SWING-DETECTION AND STRUCTURE SYSTEM
---------------------------------------

Swing_Detection_Length (default 5; valid 1..50)
  A pivot high must be strictly higher than every high in the N bars before
  and N bars after it. A pivot low must be strictly lower than every low in
  that same window. Because N future closed bars are required, confirmation is
  delayed by N bars. Increasing N finds fewer, larger and later-confirmed
  swings; decreasing N finds more, smaller and noisier swings.

Show_Swing_Points (default true)
  Shows or hides HH, LH, LL, and HL text and the dotted LAST_HIGH/LAST_LOW
  segments. It does not alter pivot detection, break detection, bias, alerts,
  or tradeability.

Classification and state
  * Accepted structure legs alternate high, low, high, low. If multiple pivot
    highs are confirmed before a pivot low, they belong to one leg and only
    the highest is retained; multiple lows before a high similarly retain only
    the lowest. This extreme-point filter prevents a nearby, less-extreme low
    from being mislabeled HL immediately after an LL (and the mirrored HH/LH
    case), which could otherwise manufacture a false CHoCH.
  * The first high and first low in the replay window are deliberately
    untyped because no prior same-side pivot exists.
  * After an intervening low leg, a high above the previous high-leg extreme
    is HH; otherwise it is LH. Equality is therefore treated as LH.
  * After an intervening high leg, a low below the previous low-leg extreme is
    LL; otherwise it is HL. Equality is therefore treated as HL.
  * last_high/last_low and their times store the newest confirmed levels.
  * high_broken/low_broken allow each level to generate at most one break.
  * A newly confirmed pivot resets the corresponding broken flag.

Break classification
  * Close above a typed HH -> bullish BOS and bullish structure.
  * In bearish structure, a close above a typed LH starts a potential bullish
    transition. Bullish CHoCH is confirmed only after a subsequent HL forms.
  * Close below a typed LL -> bearish BOS and bearish structure.
  * In bullish structure, a close below a typed HL starts a potential bearish
    transition. Bearish CHoCH is confirmed only after a subsequent LH forms.
  * Until that post-break corrective pivot is confirmed, direction remains the
    prior trend and no CHoCH is drawn, reported, or alerted. A continuation BOS
    in the prior direction cancels the pending transition.
  * An untyped first pivot cannot be broken for a signal.
  * The bullish and bearish break tests are independent. Candidate reversals
    still require their later corrective pivot before either can become the
    stored CHoCH direction/event.

Outputs
  Structure_Timeframe events set dashboard market bias, participate in
  tradeability, and can produce alerts. Optional text and level segments are
  independently reconstructed from the active chart timeframe, so changing
  chart periods still reveals that period's more or less detailed structure.
  The displayed history spans the same elapsed time as Bars_To_Process: 100 H1
  structure bars, for example, draw 400 M15 chart bars. Crucially,
  MA/session/ADX/ATR filters do not suppress historical BOS or CHoCH drawing
  and do not suppress alerts: they describe whether the setup direction passes
  qualification, while structure events remain facts of price action.


3. BOS, CHoCH, LABEL, AND LINE DISPLAY SYSTEM
---------------------------------------------

Show_BOS_Labels / Show_CHoCH_Labels (defaults true)
  Control drawing of the corresponding signal. Turning one off suppresses both
  its text and its structure segment because DrawSignal exits immediately. It
  does not suppress event classification, market bias, tradeability, or alert.

Bullish_BOS_Color / Bearish_BOS_Color (defaults teal/red)
Bullish_CHoCH_Color / Bearish_CHoCH_Color (defaults lime/magenta)
  Set text and segment color for each direction/type; visual effect only.

Label_Size (default ROAD_SMALL)
  Font size enumeration: TINY=7, SMALL=9, NORMAL=11, LARGE=14. It affects
  BOS/CHoCH and swing-point text, but not fixed-size Market High/Low labels.

Show_Structure_Lines (default true)
  Draws a horizontal segment from the broken pivot to its confirming candle.
  It has no analytical effect. It only applies when that event's label display
  is also enabled.

Line_Style (default STYLE_DASH) / Line_Width (default 1)
  Style the BOS/CHoCH segments. Width is clamped at drawing time to 1..4;
  changing either is visual only.

Result
  Bullish labels are anchored below the confirming candle at its low; bearish
  labels are anchored above it at its high. Object IDs include event type,
  direction, and confirming time to avoid duplicates within a rebuild.


4. LOWER-TIMEFRAME MOVING-AVERAGE QUALIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------

Use_MA_Filter (default true)
  Enables an MA calculated on Structure_Timeframe and directional qualification.
  Disabling it makes both long and short LTF tests pass and reports LTF MA OFF.

MA_Length (default 50; valid 1 or greater)
  Number of closes used by the MA. Larger values make a smoother, slower filter;
  smaller values respond faster and change qualification more often.

MA_Type (default ROAD_EMA)
  ROAD_EMA weights recent closes more heavily and reacts faster; ROAD_SMA gives
  each close equal weight and is generally smoother/slower at the same length.

MA_Filter_Mode (default ROAD_PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW)
  PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW: long passes when structure close > MA; short passes when
  close < MA. Equality passes neither direction.
  FULL_BODY_CLOSE: long requires open and close above MA plus a bullish candle;
  short requires open and close below MA plus a bearish candle. This is stricter
  and normally produces fewer passing setups.

Show_MA_Line (default false) / MA_Color (default blue)
  When the filter is enabled, draws up to the latest 500 bars as solid width-2
  segments. Show_MA_Line does nothing if Use_MA_Filter is false. Color and
  visibility are visual only.

Result
  The LTF state contributes to bull/bear BOS and CHoCH filter results displayed
  on the dashboard. It does not change detection/drawing/alerts.


5. HIGHER-TIMEFRAME MOVING-AVERAGE QUALIFICATION
------------------------------------------------

Use_HTF_MA_Filter (default true)
  Enables directional confirmation from a separate timeframe. Disabling it
  makes both HTF direction tests pass and reports HTF MA OFF.

HTF_Timeframe (default PERIOD_H1)
  Timeframe for the HTF MA and, in full-body mode, its candle open/close. A
  higher selection changes more slowly and filters more short-lived moves; a
  lower selection reacts sooner. Unlike the other timeframe helpers, this
  input is used directly; PERIOD_CURRENT is handled by MT5 as the current chart
  period for the indicator/series calls.

HTF_MA_Length (default 100; valid 1 or greater)
HTF_MA_Type (default ROAD_EMA)
  Control smoothing exactly like their LTF counterparts, but on HTF bars.

HTF_MA_Filter_Mode (default ROAD_PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW)
  PRICE_ABOVE_BELOW compares the structure candle close with the HTF MA.
  FULL_BODY_CLOSE requires the completed HTF candle's open and close on the
  correct side of its MA and the HTF candle color to match the direction.

Lookahead protection
  When a structure candle lies inside an HTF candle that was not yet complete,
  Road shifts to the preceding completed HTF candle. This avoids historical
  future leakage. If HTF data cannot be obtained while enabled, both HTF
  directions stay false and qualification is blocked for that structure bar.

Show_HTF_MA_Line (default false) / HTF_MA_Color (default orange)
  Draw up to 500 bars of the completed-value HTF MA as solid width-2 segments.
  They require the filter to be enabled and affect presentation only.


6. SESSION QUALIFICATION SYSTEM
-------------------------------

Use_Session_Filter (default false)
  When false all bars pass and the dashboard reports OFF. When true, the
  confirming candle's server timestamp is converted to the selected session's
  local clock and tested against a half-open [start, end) interval.

Session_Preset (default ROAD_NEW_YORK)
  NEW_YORK: 09:30-16:00 at fixed UTC-5 (offset -300 minutes).
  LONDON:    08:00-17:00 at UTC+0.
  TOKYO:     09:00-15:00 at UTC+9 (+540).
  SYDNEY:    08:00-17:00 at UTC+10 (+600).
  CUSTOM:    uses Custom_Session and Custom_UTC_Offset_Minutes.
  24X7:      every timestamp passes.

  Preset offsets are fixed: daylight-saving time is not automatically applied.
  Users must adjust relevant offsets/configuration when seasonal time changes
  matter.

Custom_Session (default "0930-1600")
  CUSTOM-only HHMM-HHMM interval. Invalid shape or invalid hours/minutes makes
  every checked bar fail. Overnight ranges are supported (for example,
  2200-0600). Equal start/end means all day.

Custom_UTC_Offset_Minutes (default 0)
  UTC offset of the custom session clock. Positive values are east of UTC.

Server_UTC_Offset_Minutes (default 0)
  UTC offset of broker server timestamps. Conversion is:
    local time = server time + (session offset - server offset).
  A wrong value shifts the tested window and can incorrectly pass/block bars.

Result
  Session pass is combined with both MA directions. It affects dashboard filter
  qualification only, not structure drawing or alerts.


7. ADX STRENGTH QUALIFICATION SYSTEM
------------------------------------

Use_ADX_Filter (default true)
  Enables the main ADX buffer on Structure_Timeframe. Disabling it passes all
  ADX tests and reports OFF.

ADX_Length (default 14; valid 1 or greater)
  ADX lookback. A shorter length reacts more quickly and erratically; a longer
  length is smoother and slower. ADX measures strength, not bull/bear direction.

ADX_Minimum (default 25.0)
  A bar passes when ADX is valid and >= this value. Raising it requires stronger
  trends and blocks more setups; lowering it admits weaker/ranging conditions.
  No explicit range validation is performed for this threshold.

Apply_ADX_Filter_To (default ROAD_BOS_ONLY)
  BOS_ONLY: ADX applies to BOS qualification; CHoCH ignores ADX.
  BOS_AND_CHOCH: both event types require the ADX pass.
  ATR, MA, and session requirements remain independent.

Result
  ADX status and scope appear on the dashboard and feed its BOS/CHoCH filter
  rows. They do not suppress confirmed event drawings or alerts.


8. ATR VOLATILITY QUALIFICATION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------

Use_ATR_Filter (default true)
  Enables ATR on Structure_Timeframe. Disabling it passes volatility checks and
  reports OFF. The separate trend-zone system can still use an ATR handle when
  trendline zones are enabled.

ATR_Length (default 14; valid 1 or greater)
  ATR lookback. Shorter reacts faster to volatility changes; longer is smoother.
  The same length is also used for trend-zone ATR on Boundary_Timeframe.

ATR_Filter_Mode (default ROAD_ATR_MINIMUM)
  MINIMUM: passes ATR >= ATR_Minimum.
  MAXIMUM: passes ATR <= ATR_Maximum.
  RANGE:   passes ATR_Minimum <= ATR <= ATR_Maximum.

ATR_Minimum (default 1.0) / ATR_Maximum (default 10.0)
  Raw symbol-price values, not points, pips, or percentages. Increasing the
  minimum rejects quieter bars; decreasing it admits them. Decreasing the
  maximum rejects more volatile bars; increasing it admits them. In RANGE mode,
  a minimum above the maximum makes all valid ATR values fail. Thresholds have
  no explicit initialization validation.

Result
  ATR qualification applies to both BOS and CHoCH dashboard filter results,
  regardless of ADX scope. It does not suppress event drawing or alerts.


9. HIGHER-TIMEFRAME MARKET HIGH / MARKET LOW SYSTEM
---------------------------------------------------

Show_HTF_Support_Resistance (default true)
  Enables horizontal market-boundary discovery and drawing. Disabling it has no
  effect on structure-timeframe structure.

Boundary_Timeframe (default PERIOD_H4)
  Timeframe used to find boundary pivots. Higher values create broader, slower
  boundaries; lower values create more local boundaries.

Boundary_Lookback_Bars (default 50; valid 1..100000)
  Number of recent closed SR bars searched. Older padding is loaded only so a
  pivot near the start can be confirmed. A larger lookback can find more distant
  extremes but costs processing; the chosen Market High is the highest
  confirmed pivot high and Market Low is the lowest confirmed pivot low in the
  actual lookback.

SR_Pivot_Length (default 3; valid 1..20)
  Number of closed SR bars required on both sides of a strict pivot. Increasing
  it selects fewer/more prominent boundaries and increases confirmation delay;
  decreasing it accepts more local levels.

SR_Line_Style (default STYLE_DOT) / SR_Line_Width (default 2)
  Visual style. Width is clamped to 1..4 by the common segment drawer.

Show_SR_Labels (default true)
  Toggles fixed-size (8) "Market High" and "Market Low" labels only. Boundary
  lines remain when false.

Result
  Market High is drawn only if above the current bid (or latest closed structure
  close if no usable bid); Market Low only if below it. Each black horizontal
  level starts at its pivot and rays right. A boundary on the wrong side of
  current price is omitted rather than relabeled. These levels do not affect
  signal, filter, bias, tradeability, or alert calculations. The levels are
  still measured by the Optimal Conditions Meter when its display overlay is
  disabled.


10. TRENDLINE-ZONE SYSTEM
-------------------------

Show_Trendline_Zones (default true)
  Enables one nearest valid resistance zone and one nearest valid support zone.
  It also controls creation of the trend-timeframe ATR handle.

Boundary_Timeframe (default PERIOD_H4)
  Source timeframe; PERIOD_CURRENT resolves to the chart timeframe. Higher
  values create broader and slower zones; lower values create more local zones.

Trendline_Bars_To_Apply (default 300; valid 50..100000)
  Historical region searched for trend pivots. Larger values provide more line
  candidates and processing cost; smaller values focus on recent structure.

Trendline_Pivot_Source (default ROAD_TREND_HIGH_LOW)
  HIGH_LOW uses highs for resistance pivots and lows for support pivots.
  CLOSE uses closes for both, generally reducing wick influence and moving zone
  anchors toward candle bodies.

Trendline_Pivot_Strength (default 10; valid 5..15)
  Strict bars-on-each-side pivot window. Higher strength yields fewer, more
  prominent and later-confirmed anchors; lower strength yields more candidates.

Trendline_Min_Pivot_Confirmation (default 3; valid 2..8)
  Minimum candidate pivots that must touch a proposed line within the threshold.
  Raising it makes zones rarer and more strongly corroborated; lowering it makes
  them easier to form. Runtime also clamps this value to 2..8.

Trendline_Resistance_Color (default red)
Trendline_Support_Color (default green)
Trendline_Zone_Transparency (default 50; valid 0..100)
  Visual settings. Transparency 0 is opaque and 100 is invisible. Boundary
  lines use a fixed 50% transparency derived from the chosen color.

Algorithm and result
  * Road loads closed trend-timeframe rates plus pivot padding.
  * A fixed, non-input tolerance of 0.5 times the latest trend-timeframe ATR is
    used for both touch matching and break rejection.
  * Every pair of pivots defines a candidate slope. Later pivots within the ATR
    tolerance count as touches and set the zone's observed upper/lower spread.
  * A resistance candidate is rejected if an eligible older pivot exceeds it by
    more than tolerance; support is rejected for the opposite violation. The
    newest five bars form a stability buffer and do not invalidate a candidate.
  * Among candidates meeting the touch minimum, the projected line closest to
    the latest trend close wins independently for resistance and support.
  * The output is a filled, right-rayed sloped channel plus right-rayed top and
    bottom boundaries. Zones do not create structure signals, but their latest
    projected bounds are used by the Optimal Conditions Meter. Zone discovery
    therefore continues when the overlay is hidden and the meter is enabled.

ATR_Length indirectly changes the zone tolerance. Larger/smoother ATR windows
usually make its width/tolerance less reactive; higher current ATR expands the
allowed touch/break distance, while lower ATR tightens it.


11. MARKET BIAS AND TRADEABILITY SYSTEM
------------------------------------------

Road independently replays confirmed structure on Structure_Timeframe (HTF),
Setup_Entry_Timeframe (MTF), and LTF_Timeframe. The HTF bias is classified as
follows:

  Bullish:              latest break is bullish BOS and latest pivots are HH/HL.
  Bullish (Transition): bullish direction exists, but the latest break is CHoCH
                        or the latest pivot pair is not yet a clean HH/HL.
  Bearish:              latest break is bearish BOS and latest pivots are LH/LL.
  Bearish (Transition): bearish direction exists, but the latest break is CHoCH
                        or the latest pivot pair is not yet a clean LH/LL.
  Consolidating:        no confirmed directional break exists in replay history.

The MTF and LTF use the event sequence directly: each is transitional
only when its latest break is a CHoCH. The first subsequent BOS completes the
transition, and later pivot classifications cannot make it transitional again
unless another CHoCH occurs.

The enabled tradeability timeframe biases correlate when they all point in the
same direction. The HTF and MTF may be established or transitional. If LTF is
enabled, it must be definite, meaning its most recent break is a BOS. Thus the
defaults retain the original all-three behavior, while disabling LTF permits
HTF/MTF correlation and enabling only HTF permits directional HTF structure to
stand alone. A CHoCH makes an enabled LTF transitional until a subsequent BOS,
regardless of later pivot classifications.

Market Tradeability prints Tradable when the selected biases meet those rules.
Optimal Conditions remains a separate quality meter and does not override
timeframe tradeability. Otherwise Tradeability prints Not Tradable and
identifies unavailable, consolidating, conflicting, or transitional selected
data.


12. DASHBOARD SYSTEM
--------------------

Bold chart labels display the HTF, MTF, and LTF market biases, Market Tradeability and
its reason, plus the Optimal Conditions result, Definite Bias, Technical Space,
Healthy Extension, Market Volume, Price Momentum, and the optimal-condition
reason. Labels use the chart foreground color so they remain readable when the
chart theme changes. Detailed session, indicator, swing, and aggregate-filter
diagnostics remain part of the internal calculation but no longer clutter the
chart.


12A. OPTIMAL CONDITIONS METER
-----------------------------

The meter is always enabled as an internal calculation. OPTIMAL is printed only
when all five requirements below pass simultaneously. Its detailed tuning
values retain their documented defaults but are intentionally hidden from the
normal Inputs dialog.

Definite Market Bias
  Requires directional correlation between the enabled tradeability
  timeframes. HTF and MTF biases may be established or transitional. When LTF
  is enabled, its latest break must be a BOS; a CHoCH remains transitional
  until a later BOS confirms continuation.

Clear Technical Space
  The latest price must be farther than Boundary_Clearance_ATR (default 1.0)
  LTF ATR from every available higher-timeframe Market High,
  Market Low, resistance trendline zone, and support trendline zone. Being
  inside a trendline zone also blocks it. The dashboard identifies the first
  nearby boundary. A missing/invalid ATR blocks the requirement.

Healthy Extension
  In a bullish trend, extension is measured from the newest corrective low to
  the latest close; in a bearish trend it is measured from the newest
  corrective high. The distance must be non-negative and no greater than
  Maximum_Extension_ATR (default 3.0) ATR. Strict tradeability independently
  ensures that the anchor is an HL or LH, so both a correction and a controlled
  post-correction run are required.

Good Market Volume
  The newest closed LTF candle's tick volume is divided by the average of the
  preceding Volume_Average_Length candles (default 20). It passes from
  Volume_Minimum_Ratio (default 0.50) through Volume_Maximum_Ratio (default
  2.00), inclusive. This rejects both inactive conditions and volume spikes.
  MT5 tick volume is used because centralized real volume is not consistently
  available across instruments.

Good Price Momentum
  Momentum is the newest closed LTF candle's true range divided by the average true
  range of the preceding Momentum_Average_Length candles (default 20). True
  range includes gaps from the preceding close, so the measure captures actual
  price travel without imposing a bullish or bearish direction. It passes from
  Momentum_Minimum_Ratio (default 0.50) through Momentum_Maximum_Ratio (default
  2.00), inclusive. Below the band, price is considered sluggish; above the
  band, price is considered too fast to enter without chasing. Volume and
  momentum must both pass, which describes active but controlled conditions
  rather than treating a volume spike alone as a suitable entry environment.

Failure reasons
  NOT OPTIMAL is accompanied by every failed category: absent continuation
  bias, the nearby boundary name, excessive/invalid extension, volume that is
  too low or too high, and momentum that is sluggish or too fast. This makes
  the final result diagnostic rather than a bare pass/fail flag.


13. ALERT SYSTEM
----------------

Enable_Popup_Alerts (default true)
  Calls MT5 Alert with "<symbol> <structure timeframe> <signal>".

Enable_Push_Notifications (default false)
  Sends the same text through SendNotification; terminal notification settings
  must also be configured for delivery.

Eligibility and deduplication
  Alerts occur only after initialization, only during a new-bar rebuild, and
  only when the newest replayed event belongs to the most recently closed bar.
  A static last-alert time prevents another alert for the same/older timestamp.
  Display toggles and all qualification filters do not gate alerts. If both
  directions occurred on one bar, the later stored bearish event is the one
  alerted. Disabling both channels performs no delivery, though the event is
  still internally considered by the alert routine.


14. INTERNAL VARIABLES AND RESOURCE LIFECYCLE
---------------------------------------------

Persistent globals
  g_prefix             Unique "Road_<chart ID>_" object-name prefix.
  g_last_ltf_bar       Current open LTF bar time used for refresh gating.
  g_last_structure_bar Current open structure bar time used for refresh gating.
  g_last_setup_bar     Current open setup bar time used for refresh gating.
  g_ma_handle          Structure-timeframe MA handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_htf_ma_handle      HTF MA indicator handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_adx_handle         Structure-timeframe ADX handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_atr_handle         Structure-timeframe ATR handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_trend_atr_handle   Trend-timeframe ATR handle, or INVALID_HANDLE.
  g_ltf_atr_handle     LTF ATR handle used by the conditions meter.

Key rebuild-local variables
  rates/ma/adx/atr     Closed-bar price and enabled indicator histories.
  have_high/have_low   Whether a confirmed pivot of each side exists.
  last_high_kind       +1 HH, -1 LH, 0 untyped.
  last_low_kind        +1 LL, -1 HL, 0 untyped.
  high_broken/low_broken
                       One-signal-per-current-pivot guards.
  structure            +1 bullish, -1 bearish, 0 undefined.
  last_break_direction +1 up, -1 down, 0 none.
  last_break_was_bos   Distinguishes continuation from transition.
  newest_signal/time   Candidate alert text and confirming timestamp.
  dashboard_*          Latest-bar qualification snapshots.

Changing globals directly is not a supported user control; they are derived
state. On initialization, Road validates bounded inputs, creates only required
indicator handles, starts a two-second timer, and attempts a non-alerting build.
If history or indicator data are still synchronizing (as commonly happens just
after a chart timeframe change), the incomplete build retains the prior display
and does not consume the current bar; the timer retries it every two seconds
until all required data are ready. On deinitialization Road kills the timer,
releases every created handle, deletes only prefixed objects, and clears the
chart comment.


15. PRACTICAL INTERACTION MAP
-----------------------------

  Change structure frequency:
    Structure_Timeframe, Swing_Detection_Length, Bars_To_Process.

  Change whether the latest directional setup qualifies:
    MA and HTF-MA settings, session settings, ADX settings, ATR settings.

  Change the Optimal Conditions result:
    Incoming clean structure, Boundary_Clearance_ATR,
    Maximum_Extension_ATR, Volume_Average_Length, the volume ratio bounds,
    Momentum_Average_Length, and the momentum ratio bounds.

  Change the structural TRADEABLE result:
    Only incoming confirmed pivot/break sequence (indirectly affected by the
    structure timeframe, swing length, and available replay history).

  Change boundary overlays:
    Boundary_Timeframe and SR settings for horizontal Market High/Low; trendline
    settings and ATR_Length
    for sloped support/resistance zones.


  Change visuals only:
    Show toggles, colors, label size, line styles/widths, transparency.

  Change notifications only:
    Enable_Popup_Alerts and Enable_Push_Notifications.

No parameter turns Road into an automated trading system. Its results are chart
objects, dashboard text, and optional notifications; execution decisions and
risk management remain outside this EA.
